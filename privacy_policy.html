Her bağlantıda IP adresin teknik olarak altyapı sağlayıcısının kayıtlarına kısa süreliğine düşebilir. Diğer oyuncular yalnızca adını (12 yaş altıysan 'OYUNCU-XXXX'), profil resmini, çerçeveni, seviyeni ve skorlarını görür; oyuncu ID'ni sadece arkadaşların görür.

3. Reklamlar (Google AdMob): reklam göstermek, ölçmek ve dolandırıcılığı önlemek için Google reklam kimliğini, IP adresini, cihaz ve işletim sistemi bilgisini, yaklaşık konumu ve reklam etkileşimlerini işleyebilir. Ayar seçtiğin yaş grubuna göre yapılır: 12 yaş ve altı için reklamlar çocuklara yönelik işaretlenir, kişiselleştirilmez ve yalnızca genel izleyiciye uygun içerik istenir; 13-17 yaş reşit olmayan kullanıcı olarak işaretlenir. İzin gereken bölgelerde (ör. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık) önce Google'ın izin formu gösterilir; seçimini Ayarlar > REKLAM İZİNLERİ'nden değiştirebilirsin (bu düğme yalnızca izin gereken yerlerde görünür). Reklam kimliğini telefonunun ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir ya da silebilirsin. Koşu sırasında reklam gösterilmez; ödüllü videolar yalnızca sen bir düğmeye basınca açılır.

Oyun yalnızca ürün kimliğini, satın alma durumunu ve Google'ın satın alma onayını alır; bunları ürünü vermek ve kalıcı ürünleri (özel karakterler, tek seferlik paketler) yeniden yüklemek için kullanır.

5. Google Play Oyunlar ile giriş (isteğe bağlı): Ayarlar'da GOOGLE İLE GİRİŞ YAP düğmesi görünüyorsa ve giriş yaparsan, Google'ın verdiği oyuncu kimliği ve oyuncu adı kullanılır, oyun kaydın Google'ın Kayıtlı Oyunlar hizmetine yedeklenir. Flyway: Flying Car Race — Privacy Policy

Data controller: Yalçın Studio (developer of Flyway). Contact: yalcinstudio.flyway@gmail.com. Last updated: 28 October 2026. This policy describes the features that actually work in this version of the game; a feature you do not see in your game (e.g. Google sign-in) does not apply to you.

1. Stored on your phone: your game progress (coins, gems, cars, characters, outfits, missions, achievements, records, level), your settings (sound, vibration, left-handed mode, language), your player name, the age group you picked at first launch, your player ID and its secret key. These stay only in the save file on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the game or use Settings > DELETE MY ACCOUNT.

2. Stored on our server (leaderboard and friends): if these screens work in your game, the following is sent to our server (Supabase infrastructure) while you are online: your player ID, player name, chosen profile picture and frame, level, whether you are under 13, your score, distance and time in each mode, your friends list, friend requests, players you blocked and the inappropriate-name reports you send. Your secret key is stored on the server only as a one-way hash. No e-mail, phone number, real name, location or contacts are collected. With every connection your IP address may briefly appear in the infrastructure provider's technical logs. Other players only see your name ('OYUNCU-XXXX' if you are under 13), profile picture, frame, level and scores; only your friends see your player ID.

3. Ads (Google AdMob): to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may process the advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, approximate location and ad interactions. Settings depend on the age group you chose: for 12 and under, ads are tagged as child-directed, are not personalised and only general-audience content is requested; 13-17 is tagged as under the age of consent. Where consent is required (e.g. the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom), Google's consent form is shown first; you can change your choice in Settings > AD CONSENT (this button only appears where consent is required). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings. No ads are shown during a run; rewarded videos only start when you tap a button.

4. Purchases (Google Play): payments are handled by Google Play; your card and payment details never reach us. The game only receives the product ID, the purchase state and Google's purchase confirmation, and uses them to deliver the item and to restore permanent items (special characters, one-time packs).

5. Sign-in with Google Play Games (optional): if a SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE button appears in Settings and you sign in, the player ID and player name given by Google are used and your save is backed up to Google's Saved Games service. If the button does not appear, this feature is off in your version and your progress is only on your phone; if you lose the phone or delete the game, progress does not come back (except permanent purchases).

6. Purposes and legal bases: running the game, delivering what you buy, providing the leaderboard and friends (performance of a contract); personalised ads (your consent where required); ad measurement, preventing cheating and abuse, security (legitimate interest). These bases are assessed under KVKK art. 5 and GDPR art. 6.

7. Third parties and international transfers: we do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the companies that provide the service: Google (AdMob, Google Play, optional Google Play Games) and Supabase (leaderboard server, Frankfurt data centre in the European Union). Their servers may be outside Türkiye and your country; transfers rely on safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses these providers offer.

8. Retention: the save on your phone until you delete it; your player and score data on the server until you delete your account; friend requests until accepted or rejected; name reports at most 7 days; rate-limit records 2 days; run tickets 1 day. Data processed by Google is kept for Google's own periods. The infrastructure provider's technical logs and backups may remain for their own short periods.

9. Deleting your account and data: Settings > DELETE MY ACCOUNT asks for a two-step confirmation and cannot be undone. Your player record, scores, friendships, requests, blocks and reports on the server are deleted (without internet nothing is deleted and you are asked to try again); if you signed in with Google, your cloud save is emptied; the save on your phone is deleted and the game closes. This does not delete your Google account. Records of real-money purchases remain at Google Play: if you reinstall with the same Google account, permanent items are restored; spent gems and coins do not come back. If you cannot access the game, e-mail yalcinstudio.flyway@gmail.com with your player ID to request deletion; we delete your server data within 30 days.

10. Your rights: under KVKK art. 11 and the GDPR you can access, correct and delete your data, restrict or object to processing, ask for portability and withdraw consent. Write to yalcinstudio.flyway@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days at the latest. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your country's data protection authority.

11. Children: we ask for your age at first launch and no answer is pre-selected. If 12 or under is chosen, ads are not personalised and are tagged as child-directed, the name is hidden on the leaderboard and the friends system is off. Age limits differ between countries (e.g. 13-16 in the EU); parents can write to yalcinstudio.flyway@gmail.com about any request regarding their child's data. You can limit in-app purchases with Google Play's parental controls.

12. Security: server connections are encrypted with HTTPS; direct access to the server tables is closed and every action is verified with the player ID and secret key; requests are rate-limited. No system is perfect; if a data breach happens we will notify as required by law.

13. Changes: if this policy changes, the new text is published in the game and at the link on the Play Store page and the date is updated. We announce important changes in the game.
